# Artwork provenance and licence

All images under `public/monsters`, `public/art` and `public/boo-tique/products` were **generated on 2026-11-17 with the OpenAI image model (GPT Image 2.6 Flare) through the Wiro API**, then prepared (cropped, resized, converted, transparent cut-outs, one recolour) with local Python/Pillow scripts by **Claude Code** (Anthropic). They were **not written by the Kiro CLI**. Claude Code inspected every image and rejected or regenerated off-brief results (the rejected attempts are not in the repo), and the project owner reviewed and approved the final set.

56 image files, 5,665,049 bytes in total (README excluded).

## What was used as input

Text prompts only (below). No third-party copyrighted material, artwork, photographs, brands, logos, characters or likenesses were supplied as input, and the prompts ask for no text, no logos and no real brands. The fictional Boo-tique products are plain, unbranded objects.

## Style recipe (same wording in every monster prompt)

```
Linocut / woodcut relief print in the manner of an old almanac or tarot card. Printed in exactly three flat inks: off-white #F4F0FF, soft violet #B99BFF, pumpkin orange #FF9A3C, [pink #FF6B8A used only for the vampire's collar lining,] plus dark carved shadow areas and carved gouge lines in near-black ink #13101E. Hand-cut rough edges, slight ink misregistration, consistent medium line weight. All shapes are solid, fully opaque ink: no translucency, no see-through areas. No frame, no border, no scenery.
[PLATE] PLATE VERSION: full woodcut illustration, half figure, visible carved gouge lines and hatching at moderate density, black carved shadows, rich but readable detail.
[STAMP] SIMPLIFIED MARKER STAMP VERSION: bust only, very bold flat shapes, only a few thick carved lines, no fine hatching, a thick dark outline around the whole silhouette, strong silhouette, one large prop drawn in orange, big readable face; like a rubber stamp or playing-card pip cut by the same printmaker, legible when shrunk to 44 pixels.
Figure centred and fully inside the frame with at least 12% empty margin on every side. Friendly-spooky, not gory, not childish. No text, letters, numbers or logos.
SUBJECT: ...
```

Settings: model `openai/gpt-image-2-6-flare`, 1k, 1:1, quality medium, PNG, `background: transparent`.

### How the transparent cut-outs were made

Eight plates and nine stamps came straight from the model's transparent-background output (alpha cleaned: >=240 to 255, <8 to 0) and were checked on #F3EBDD and #13101E at full size and on a 44 px badge: no halos. The vampire plate was chroma-keyed as described above. Plates are exported as lossy WebP (quality 80 to 83, alpha quality 100), stamps as optimised PNG.

Printed in flat inks: near-black #13101E as the night ground, soft violet #B99BFF, off-white #F4F0FF and pumpkin orange #FF9A3C lantern light, with carved gouge lines, hand-cut rough edges and slight ink misregistration, consistent medium line weight. Composition 16:9: the entire LEFT THIRD is calm, almost empty dark night ground with only a few small carved stars and no objects, reserved for a headline. On the right two thirds: a generic old shop window with blank empty rectangular glass panes and a plain wooden counter shelf (no sign, no writing); a hanging orange lantern on the right casts a warm glow that reveals small friendly woodcut monsters hiding behind and peeking over the window ledge: a skeleton, a ghost, a witch, a spider and a trio of bats with wide watching eyes. Friendly-spooky, not gory, not childish. No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere, no sign lettering." The 3840x2160 output was cropped by 1.4% to remove the print-paper edge, then resized to 2400 px (WebP quality 78, JPEG quality 72, 1200 px WebP, 32 px blurred placeholder). The grain in the dark area is part of the woodcut texture, not compression blocking.

Spots (same woodcut ink list, 4:3, 1k, opaque): `no-tricks-found` (task 6252714): 'a relieved little shopkeeper ghost (solid opaque off-white sheet, big happy eyes) standing behind a small counter in front of a clean empty wooden shelf with no cobwebs and no monsters, holding up a small glowing orange lantern with a calm smile and one hand over its chest in relief'. `something-went-wrong` (task 6252727): 'a badly tangled knotted spider web with loose strands, and caught in it a broken old lantern with a cracked glass and a bent handle, flickering orange flame leaking out, with one small confused spider dangling beside it'.

## Notes for UI authors

- Monster ids: skeleton, ghost, witch, spider, mummy, werewolf, vampire, zombie, bats. Bats are the ninth monster (fake activity); the ghost covers fake scarcity only.
- Stamps are drawn for a round 44 px badge. In tests a dark fill (#28213F) with the 3 px white and 2 px #13101E rings reads best for every monster; orange fills hide the orange props, lilac fills hide the violet bodies. The zombie and the bats are the lowest-contrast on dark, so encode severity with the number chip or ring colour and add a thin light inner ring if needed.
- Hero: the left third is intentionally empty for a headline.
